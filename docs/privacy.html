Privacy Policy for lit

Last updated: June 25, 2026

lit ("the app") is a book-rating app. This policy explains what data the app does and does not collect.

Information we collect

lit does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to us. The app has no user accounts and no analytics or tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your book lists, ratings, notes, and follows are stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party. Removing the app deletes this data.

Third-party services

To let you search for books and show cover images, lit sends your search terms to the Open Library public API (operated by the Internet Archive) and loads cover images from their servers. These requests contain only the text you search for and do not include any personal identifiers. Please refer to the Internet Archive's privacy policy for how they handle requests.

Children's privacy

lit does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
