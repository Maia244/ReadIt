## Description
lit is the fun way to track and rank every book you read.

Instead of forcing you to pick a star rating, lit asks how a book felt — then
walks you through quick head-to-head comparisons against books you've already
read to build your personal ranked list, with a 0–10 score for every title.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Rank by comparison — choose "I liked it," "It was okay," or "Not for me,"
  then compare head-to-head to place each book exactly.
• Keep three lists — Read, Want to Read, and Recs.
• Search millions of real books by title, author, or genre.
• Filter everything by genre and age group (Children, Middle Grade, Young
  Adult, Adult).
• See an AI community consensus for each book — a balanced summary of what all
  readers think, plus the individual reviews behind it.
• Read a quick summary of what each book is about.
• Follow other readers and see their top books.
• Track your taste with a personal stats breakdown.

Find your next great read — and never forget what you thought of the last one.
